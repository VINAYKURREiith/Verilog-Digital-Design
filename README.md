### 🏁 100-Day Learning Progression

```text
Days 01–15
Digital Logic & Arithmetic
        ↓
Days 16–30
MUX, DEMUX, Encoders & Decoders
        ↓
Days 31–45
Flip-Flops, Registers & Counters
        ↓
Days 46–55
Memory, FIFO, FSM & RTL Structures
        ↓
Days 56–65
Sequence Detectors, Generators & Arbiters
        ↓
Days 66–77
Digital Systems & Advanced Multipliers
        ↓
Days 78–87
ROM, RAM, SRAM, DRAM & Non-Volatile Memory
        ↓
Days 88–95
Algorithmic RTL & Advanced FIFO
        ↓
Days 96–100
Advanced RTL Design & Flash Memory
```

### 🎯 What This 100-Day Challenge Covers

By completing the full roadmap, the repository covers:

* **Combinational Logic Design**
* **Arithmetic Circuit Design**
* **Multiplexer & Demultiplexer Design**
* **Encoder & Decoder Design**
* **Code Converters**
* **Comparator Design**
* **Flip-Flops**
* **Registers & Shift Registers**
* **Counter Design**
* **ALU Design**
* **Finite State Machines**
* **Mealy & Moore Machines**
* **Sequence Detectors**
* **Sequence Generators**
* **FIFO & LIFO**
* **Priority Arbiters**
* **RAM & ROM**
* **SRAM & DRAM**
* **PROM & EPROM**
* **PAL & PLA**
* **Digital System Controllers**
* **Advanced Multiplier Architectures**
* **Asynchronous FIFO**
* **Flash Memory Concepts**
* **RTL-Based Algorithmic Design**

This progression is intended to build practical Verilog skills from **basic logic gates to advanced RTL and memory architectures**.
